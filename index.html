How the estimate works

Income Tax is approximated using annualised monthly pay and England/Wales/Northern Ireland bands. Employee NI is estimated using monthly thresholds. This does not model cumulative PAYE, tax-code adjustments, student loans, benefits, multiple jobs or every pension arrangement.
